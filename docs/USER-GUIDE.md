# D Discourse - User Guide

_Read · Write · Discuss_

Welcome to D Discourse, a place for focused, topic-based conversations. This guide shows you how to browse, create an account, publish articles, and join discussions.

<!-- > **Note:** D Discourse is still being developed. Some features described here may not be available in the live app yet. If a button or option mentioned in this guide is missing, it has probably not been released. -->

## 1. What is D Discourse?

Most social platforms put everything into one endless feed, where good posts get lost. D Discourse organises conversations into **boards**, and each board is about one topic, such as Technology, Education, or Health & Wellness.

Three ideas are worth knowing:

| Term        | What it means                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Board**   | A space for one topic. Articles and discussions on that topic live here.                   |
| **Article** | A post you publish inside a board to share an idea, an update, a question, or information. |
| **Comment** | A reply to an article. Comments are how people discuss it.                                 |

## 2. Getting started

### Browsing without an account

You can look around before you sign up. As a visitor you can:

- Browse the list of boards
- Open a board and see its articles
- Read any article and its comments
- Search, sort, and filter boards and articles

You **cannot** post, comment, or rate anything until you sign in.

### Creating an account

1. Open the app at **ddiscourse.runasp.net**.
2. Choose the option to **create an account** (usually a button with _Create Account_).
3. Enter your **first** and **lastname** which is required.
4. Enter a **username** and an **email address**. Both must be unique, so if one is already taken you will be asked to choose another.
5. Choose a **password** and submit the form.
6. You are now ready to sign in and take part.

Your password is stored in a protected (hashed) form. No one on the team can read it.

### Signing in and out

- To sign in, choose **Sign in** and enter your details.
- To sign out, open your account menu and choose **Sign out**. Do this on shared computers.
- On desktop, you can also sign out using the sign out button at the bottom of the side-bar.
- Signing in with an outside provider such as Google may be offered later. It is an optional feature and might not be available yet.

## 3. Finding your way around

The app works in a desktop or mobile web browser, and the layout adjusts to your screen size.

- **On a computer**, navigation is usually along the side or top of the page.
- **On a phone**, the layout is simpler and the same options are reachable from the menu.

You don't need to install anything. Open the site in any modern browser.

## 4. Boards

### Browsing boards

The boards page lists the topic spaces on the platform. At the home page, You can:

- **Search** for a board by name
- **Sort** the list, for example by newest or most active
- **Filter** to narrow down what you see

On the board page, you can not search, sort or filter board, you can only scroll through and select a board to open it and see its articles.

### Creating and managing boards

Creating and managing boards needs the right **permission level** (see section 8). If you have it:

1. Go to the boards page and choose **Create Board** or **New Board**.
2. Enter a clear **name** that describes the topic.
3. Save it.

You can delete boards **you created**, subject to your permissions. Deleting a board is a significant action, so check that you really want to do it first.

If you don't see the option to create a board, your account doesn't currently have that permission.

## 5. Articles

### Reading and finding articles

Open any article from the home page or from inside a board. You can search, sort, and filter articles to find what interests you.

### Writing an article

Any signed-in user can publish an article.

1. Sign in.
2. Choose **Write Article** (or **Create Article**) or on mobile choose the plus (**+**) icon at the bottom navigation bar.
3. Pick the **board** the article belongs to. Choose the one that best fits your topic.
4. Write a **title** and your **text**.
5. Choose **Publish**.

Your name and the publication time are shown on the article automatically.

**Tips for good articles**

- Write a clear title that says what the article is about.
- Post in the board that matches the topic, so the right readers find it.
- Keep to one main idea per article.

### Editing and deleting your articles

You can update or delete **your own** articles. Open the article and look for the edit or delete options. You cannot edit articles written by other people.

### Rating articles

Signed-in users can **rate** articles written by others. This helps good content stand out. You can't rate your own articles.

## 6. Comments and discussions

Comments are where the conversation happens.

### Reading a discussion

Scroll below an article to read its comments. Each comment shows who wrote it and when.

### Adding a comment

1. Sign in.
2. Open an article.
3. Type your response in the comment box.
4. Submit it.

### Editing and deleting your comments

You can change or remove **your own** comments at any time. You cannot change anyone else's.

### What comments don't include

To keep D Discourse simple, there is **no searching, sorting, or filtering of comments**, and **comments cannot be liked or rated**.

## 7. Your account

### Managing your account

You can manage your account details from your account or profile area.
A profile photo is optional (_not currently available_).

### Deleting your account

You can delete your own account at any time. Before you do:

- Deleting is intended to be **permanent**.
- Consider whether you want to remove your articles and comments first.
- Contact the team if you are unsure what happens to your content.

## 8. Permission levels

What you can do depends on your account type.

| Who                              | What they can do                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Visitor** (not signed in)      | Browse boards, read articles and comments, and search, sort, and filter.                                                    |
| **Contributor / signed-in user** | Everything a visitor can do, plus write articles, post comments, rate articles, and manage their own articles and comments. |
| **Moderator**                    | Has extra permissions to help manage content and discussions.                                                               |
| **Administrator**                | Has the highest level of permissions and helps manage the community.                                                        |

The exact actions available to moderators and administrators depend on how the app is set up. Boards are created and managed by users with the right permission level.

## 9. Community etiquette

Good boards depend on good habits. Please:

- **Stay on topic.** Post in the board that fits.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Share honestly.** Give credit when you use someone else's work.
- **Ask questions.** Questions are welcome and often start the best discussions.

Moderators and administrators may remove content that harms the community.

## 10. Privacy and security

- Passwords are stored in a hashed form.
- If you use an outside sign-in provider, that provider's security applies to your login.
- Use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.
- Never share your password with anyone, including other members.

## 11. Troubleshooting and FAQ

**I can't comment or post.** You need to be signed in. Sign in or create an account first.

**My username or email is rejected when I sign up.** Both must be unique. Try another username, or sign in if you already have an account.

**I don't see a "Create Board" or "New Board" button.** Board management needs a higher permission level than a standard account.

**I can't edit someone's article or comment.** You can only edit and delete your own content.

**A feature in this guide is missing.** D Discourse is still under development, so some features may not be live yet.

**The page looks cramped on my phone.** Try refreshing the page or using a current version of your browser. The layout is built to work on mobile.
